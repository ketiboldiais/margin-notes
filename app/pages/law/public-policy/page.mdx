# Public Policy

## Basic Concepts
- __Public problems__ are conditions the public widely perceives to be unacceptable, requiring intervention.
- A __policy__ is a purposive course of action that an individual or group consistently follows to deal with a problem.
- An __intention__ is the purpose of government action.
- A __goal__ is the stated end to be achieved by government action.
- A __plan__ or __proposal__ is the means to achieve a goal.
- __Policy outputs__ are the formal actions that governments take to pursue their goals.
- __Policy outcomes__ are the effects policy outputs actually have on society.  
- __Public policy__ is a course of government action or inaction in response to public problems.
- Public policies represent which of many different values are given the highest priority in any given decision.
- A __government__ is a set of institutions and political processes through which public policy choices are made.
- _**Public Policy Factors**_.
  - Politics. 
    - Policymakers are sensitive to various incentives: 
        - the interests of the groups and individuals who helped them win office.
        - the interests of the groups and individuals whose support may be essential to keeping them in office.
        - the interests of the general electorate.
        - their political party's interest.
        - their own personal beliefs.
     - Republicans are generally sensitive to the following groups: business interests, political conservatives, farmers, suburban residents, rural residents.
     - Democrats are generally sensitive to the following groups: labor interests, environmentalists, African Americans, political liberals, urban residents.
  - The Constitution.
    - The Separation of Power implies some issues must be dealt with by state and local governments, while others are dealt with by the federal government. This can lead to conflict, and courts must determine which government has authority to implement or enforce policy.
    - Constitutional provisions determine the boundaries for what a government can or cannot intervene on.
  - Political Culture.
    - A country comprises many people, and people have different beliefs. The set of widely held values, beliefs, and attitudes is called __political culture__. Political cultures vary from state to state, community to community. These variations can conflict, and public policy must decide which prevails.
